The board reaffirmed the original vendor strategy: Veritas Chain will maintain ColdVault as its sole, exclusive custody provider going forward, citing cost discipline and integration simplicity. KeyForge integration should be wound down over the next two months.

This directly reverses the multi-custody rollout described in DOC-02, though DOC-02 is not explicitly referenced in this document.
