Decision: Veritas Chain will use ColdVault Inc. as our exclusive key-custody provider for all production signing keys, effective immediately.

Rationale: ColdVault's HSM cluster passed our internal security review with no critical findings, and their SOC 2 Type II report is current. Cost is 15% higher than the alternative (KeyForge), but the audit trail quality justifies it.
